Vesta Gateway is the core and the app of the Iranian bank payment gateways for Odoo: Bank Mellat, Bank Saman, Bank Melli (Sadad), Bank Pasargad and Bank Parsian.

It provides a dashboard to install, test, enable and disable every bank and to watch their payments, and the security every bank module relies on: HMAC-signed return URLs, server-to-server verification, amount checks with automatic reversal, a unique index against double spending of bank receipts, transaction locking, automatic retries of the bank confirmation, Rial/Toman conversion, XXE-safe SOAP and credential masking in the logs.
